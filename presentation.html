Risks & Mitigation
We've Thought It All Through
Slide 8 / 9
Rakshak KrishiNet 2.0
🛡️ Technical Risks
⚡ Power FailureMEDIUM
Mitigation: Solar-charged LiPo battery backup. RPi runs 8hr on 10,000mAh. Automatic low-power mode activates.
📶 No Internet/GSMHIGH
Mitigation: Full Edge AI is 100% offline. Detection and deterrent work without internet. SMS queued for when signal returns.
🌧️ Drone Weather GroundingMEDIUM
Mitigation: Deterrents (ultrasonic + LED) work independently of drone. Ground-based fallback covers 95% of intrusion scenarios.
🎯 AI False PositivesLOW
Mitigation: 80% confidence threshold filter. Self-learning model reduces false positives 41% over time. Farmer can override.
📋 Business & Operational Risks
💰 Affordability / AdoptionMEDIUM
Mitigation: HaaS / EMI model. ₹499/mo SaaS subscription. FPO cluster subsidy route. Govt PM-Kisan scheme integration.
📚 Tech Literacy GapMEDIUM
Mitigation: Hindi/Marathi UI. Voice alerts. Simple SMS (no app needed). Community ambassador program in each village.
📜 Drone Regulation (DGCA)HIGH
Mitigation: <250g nano-drone falls under Green Zone exemption. DGCA pre-approval pathway applicable. Ongoing regulatory compliance team.
🔒 Data PrivacyLOW
Mitigation: All video processed locally on-device. No raw footage sent to cloud. Farmer owns all their data. DPDP Act compliant.
